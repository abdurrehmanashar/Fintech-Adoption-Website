About

Independent student researcher

Interested in machine learning, financial inclusion, and technology for social impact.

I grew up in Gujranwala - a mid-sized industrial city that rarely comes up in conversations about Pakistan's tech and fintech growth. What struck me wasn't a lack of phones; almost everyone around me had one. It was watching shopkeepers, relatives, and neighbours who owned smartphones and had bank accounts they'd opened once and never touched again - still running everything in cash, still asking someone else to send money on their behalf.

That contradiction - access without engagement - is the exact gap this project set out to measure. When the data independently surfaced a "Connected but Unengaged" segment covering 44% of the sample, it didn't feel like an abstract statistic. It felt like a description of people I actually know.

Abdur-Rehman Ashar
Lead Researcher - Beaconhouse School System PTC, Gujranwala

Research interests: Machine Learning, Explainable AI, Financial Inclusion, AI for Social Good
