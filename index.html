A DATA STORY ABOUT DIGITAL FINANCE IN PAKISTAN

98% have a phone. Two-thirds still aren't banked.

Nearly half of Pakistanis have a smartphone and internet access - yet most of them still do not use digital finance at all. This is that gap, in the country's own survey data.

What the data shows

Three findings worth sitting with

Drawn from an independent analysis of 1,000 respondents in the World Bank's Global Findex 2025 survey - access, behaviour, and demographics, matched against actual financial usage.

01
44%

Access is not the bottleneck

44% of people have near-universal phone and internet access - but two-thirds of them are still formally excluded from the financial system.

02
96%

The gap has a gender

The most digitally excluded group is 96% women. Gender predicts financial exclusion more strongly than region in this dataset.
